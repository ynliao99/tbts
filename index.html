Quality

We make and serve our bubble tea drinks with premium ingredients, along with love and care. Sourcing from various regions in East Asia, each tea selection is made after numerous tasting tests. Our loose leaf tea is brewed to perfection, and we only use fresh cut fruit to bring out the simplest and clean taste of our handcrafted drinks.

Aesthetics

Enjoy the stunning world-class view of the city of Boston from our elegant deck with a handcrafted drink in hand. Our store is designed to create a space for you to relax, recharge, and rejoice all year round. And of course, we have seasonal drinks!

Diversity

Founded by people from traditional tea drinking families and cultures, Tea By The Sea hopes to bring diversity to the community so that everyone can find something he she they/whatever your pronoun is from our menu. Whether you are a pro bubble tea drinker(we hope to bring a sense of home) or a first timer, we love you and want to celebrate our culture with you.
