    When are you open?营业时间是什么？

    We are open every day from 11am to 9pm, all year round.全年每天 11:00 至 21:00 营业。

    Which entrance should I use?应该从哪个入口进店？

    Use the waterfront entrance at 99 Sumner Street facing the harborwalk. The map above points right to the building.请走 99 Sumner Street 朝向海滨步道一侧的入口，上方地图可直接导航。

    Can I change the sweetness and ice?可以调整甜度和冰量吗？

    Yes. Sweetness and ice levels are adjustable on every drink when you order online or at the counter.可以，在线点单或到店点单时，每杯饮品都能自由调整甜度和冰量。

    Do you make warm drinks?有温饮或热饮吗？

    Most milk teas, pure teas, taro drinks and coffees can be made warm or hot. Select your temperature when ordering.大部分奶茶、纯茶、芋泥和咖啡系列都可以做温饮或热饮，点单时选择温度即可。

    Can I host an event here?可以在店里办活动吗？

    Yes. We welcome birthdays, meetups, workshops and group gatherings with harbor views. Use the inquiry form above to share your date and headcount.可以。生日、社团聚会、工作坊和团体包场都欢迎，可通过上方表单或邮件告知日期和人数。

    Do you offer catering?提供饮品外送订餐（Catering）吗？

    Yes. Email us with your event details, delivery time and the drinks you have in mind.提供。请发邮件告诉我们活动时间、人数和想预订的饮品。
